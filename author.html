01 / DOMINUS

Writing at the limits of evidence

Pavel Velichii is the author of DOMINUS — The Vatican Protocol, a speculative techno-thriller set at the meeting point of archival research, artificial intelligence and theological inquiry. Its central question is not whether a machine can reproduce a human voice, but who has the authority to say that the reproduction is the final truth about a person.

02 / THE WORK

The project

DOMINUS begins with a death in the Vatican archives and a record that should not exist. Its investigation moves across historical sources, technical verification, institutional power and the ethics of testimony. Scientific uncertainty and competing interpretations remain central to the story: extraordinary claims are not presented as established historical facts.
