## EN — Book One: Schism

**One-line pitch:** A witness dies inside the Vatican archives. A record bears a date that has not yet arrived. And an AI challenges who gets the final word about a human life.

**Short description:** When a mysterious record surfaces in the Vatican archives, an investigation begins at the intersection of historical evidence, artificial intelligence and human testimony. DOMINUS asks whether technology can preserve the voice of a person without appropriating the right to define that person.

**Reader promise:** A suspense-driven intellectual thriller about memory, institutional power, faith and human agency. The narrative does not ask the reader to accept proven supernatural intervention.
