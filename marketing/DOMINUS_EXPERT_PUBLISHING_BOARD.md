## Publication release pipeline (per language × book × format)

**Stage A — Intake:** locate actual file bytes, set language, Book number, revision hash, provenance, rights and companion files. No silent regeneration.
**Stage B — Literary audit:** compare chapters to approved master; preserve full dialogs and lore, verify continuity, names, Latin grammar with translations, translation quality and no invented scenes.
**Stage C — Production QA:** EPUB navigation, images and embedded font rights; print trim/bleed, final pagination, ISBN and spine, correct cover version, print and Kindle previews.
**Stage D — KDP validation:** reconcile existing Bookshelf item and publication status, contributor/title/series/keywords/countries/prices, necessary AI-generated content disclosure and KDP Select restrictions; no duplicate books.
**Stage E — Human release approval:** author signs off exact artefacts, descriptions and costs. Do not click Publish, grant permissions, order proofs or spend without clear authority.
**Stage F — 30-day optimization:** validate live listing, format linkage, author pages, purchase links, qualified reader response, advertising economics, next-volume read-through.

**Hard stop / no-go:** missing original or translation rights, obvious skipped chapters, illegible print, inconsistent names/series volume, KDP rejection, unverified ISBN, uncontrolled expenditure.

## Sales intelligence: weekly scorecard

For every format and marketplace record **the period, currency, source, timestamp, ASIN/ISBN, verified LIVE status**. Reports must separate:
- orders and units vs pages read (KU) vs royalty estimates and actual deposits;
- organic / paid traffic where attributable;
- impressions, clicks, CPC, CTR, ad-attributed orders/revenue, ACOS, spend;
- retail price and net author royalty per sale (print manufacturing, royalty tier, delivery cost and taxes may affect returns);
- contribution = attributable incremental royalty - ad spend - other variable cost;
- series read-through = eligible Book II readers/orders relative to Book I cohort, subject to available reporting.

Calculate break-even ACOS from **author royalty ÷ selling price** for an attributable retail order (use format/territory-specific real numbers); never blindly use Amazon ad sales revenue as author profit. For cross-country comparisons keep EUR/USD spending conversions with date and rate.
