## Recommended release gating

1. **G0 rights and edition identity:** canonical spelling, language locale (especially pt-BR vs pt-PT), rights, series numbering, publication ISBN strategy, territorial rights, KDP AI-generated content declaration.
2. **G1 translation manuscript audit:** complete chapters, no omissions or hallucinated passages, editorial and literary accuracy, glossary of theology/AI terminology, consistency of Latin and characters, title and series metadata.
3. **G2 production audit:** Kindle EPUB validated in Kindle Previewer, reflowable navigation and TOC, print PDF interior, trim/bleed/margins, cover front/back/spine correct for final page count, print proof pass.
4. **G3 marketplace preparation:** product description and A+ text, keywords, categories, search conventions, author page, price and royalty calculations, print costs, approved promotional materials.
5. **G4 controlled launch:** Kindle first, then paperback, then hardcover only when quality/profitability justified. Confirm new ASIN/ISBN in KDP before public site/press-kit updates; avoid duplicate Bookshelf titles.
6. **G5 measured scaling:** 30-day per-market baseline, avoid committing paid acquisition costs without actual conversion/royalty data; coordinate Book II to improve series read-through.
