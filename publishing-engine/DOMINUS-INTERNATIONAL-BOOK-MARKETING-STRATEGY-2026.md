# DOMINUS — International Book Marketing Strategy 2026–2027

Status: adopted operating strategy, 2026-11-14. Owner: Pavel Velichii.
Purpose: grow verified organic readers and sustainable book revenue without personal advertising expenditure.

## 1. Decision hierarchy

1. Reader-market fit and canonical, accurate book promise.
2. Verified, localized retail product page and readable sample.
3. Author-owned discoverability and permission-based audience.
4. Trust: authentic reader discussions, ARCs, fair reviews, bookseller and independent editorial mentions.
5. Release cadence and sequel read-through; count *verified* purchases, royalties and cash receipts separately.
6. Paid acquisition only after royalties have actually arrived, funds remain after tax/obligations, and author approves each campaign budget.

## 2. Market positioning and tests (hypotheses, not data-backed sales claims)

Primary promise: A Vatican archive mystery turns into a techno-thriller about AI, human testimony, and who decides the truth.

Segment A: readers of historical/religious conspiracy and archive mysteries, compare taste and metadata against Umberto Eco/Dan Brown (do not claim equivalence or endorsement).
Segment B: readers of scientific/AI philosophical suspense, compare reader expectations against Peter Watts/Michael Crichton.
Segment C: readers of intellectually rigorous speculative fiction and theological/ethical debates.
Each segment gets different content hooks; measure downstream clicks to verified retail links, not likes only.

Editorial guardrails: Vatican not endorsing; no fake religious or historical proof; Book One supernatural ambiguity stays unresolved; no invented dialogue or spoilers; preserve canon and translations; never imply German edition available before verified store listing.

## 3. Organic funnel by language

Awareness: Instagram/Facebook, accurate reviews, Author.Today, indie book sites and appropriate nonfiction/theology/AI discussion channels.
Interest: thematic hooks, spoiler-free characters, genuine author notes on research, sample from a licensed published edition (not fabricated).
Consideration: official https://pavelvialiki84-afk.github.io/dominus-vatican-protocol/read.html page, with English, Russian, German availability statements.
Conversion: verified retailer pages (EN Google Play/Kobo/bol; RU Author.Today and Google Play; DE only confirmed Amazon ASIN when live).
Retention: optional GDPR-compliant, double-opt-in reader newsletter with original bonus material and sequel updates; retailer follows, Goodreads, BookBub. Do not start data capture before privacy notices, proof of consent, retention/unsubscribe handling, and a suitable provider are configured.

CTA strategy: one action per asset; site link in Instagram bio, product link in Facebook when useful; preserve source and campaign tagging using UTM when analytics is configured. Purchase conversion usually not directly observable on third-party retailers; distinguish outbound clicks from attributed paid sales.

## 4. Top priorities and activation gates

### Amazon and retail listing QA
- Review English metadata: book hook, cover legibility, synopsis clarity, categories, seven KDP keyword slots, sample, author page and series relation; test with real comparable listings, do not stuff keywords.
- Author Central: claim/verify author page, add exact live titles and language-specific biography, no false reviews.
- Amazon A+ Content only for eligible live/preorder title, with rights-cleared art and no price/discount/Buy Now/customer reviews inside A+ modules.
- Confirm German Kindle listing and ASIN before changing campaign copy; monitor book pages and site CTA.
- Avoid KDP Select enrollment for the same digital work while its eBook is lawfully distributed via Google Play/Kobo and other digital channels. Select imposes digital exclusivity for 90 days; review title-specific agreement before any changes.

### Reader-list asset
- Draft a free original, edited 'DOMINUS CASE FILE' as a 3–5 page spoiler-free companion or an explicitly licensed excerpt, in EN initially, localize when checked.
- Stage newsletter copy + privacy policy and double opt-in; actual setup needs eligible provider and user confirmation. Never scrape emails or cold-email reviewers en masse.
- One welcome email and at most twice-monthly value-based updates once opted in.

### Trust and discovery
- Claim Goodreads author profile and free BookBub author profile after confirming identity/book listing. BookBub new release alerts depend on conditions (at least five followers, US eligibility, timely title addition).
- Build targeted list of up to 30 relevant, *real* reviewers/podcasts/curators with legitimate submission rules; do not send unsolicited bulk messages.
- Offer ARCs only under platform policy: never require, pay for, or influence reviews.
- Run organic Author.Today reader discussions consistent with community rules. Do not spam groups or use paid placement without author approval.
- BookBub Featured Deals are not necessarily free: require at least 50% discount, limited window, eligible retailers and editorial selection; defer until cash-funded campaign approvals.

### Social editorial engine
- Facebook: three existing planned posts Nov 16/18/20 2026; verify live outcomes.
- Instagram: nine 1080x1350 PNGs prepared, NOT yet scheduled; need public image URLs or attachment-backed mediaFiles. Use true AI-generated label when applicable.
- Cadence cap 3 organic posts/week per network. Week themes: mystery/plot, scientific/ethical problem, research and author world. Audience languages EN/DE/RU balanced by verified retail status.
- Avoid posting same image and text repeatedly; measure saves, profile visits, link clicks and follows, plus attributable downstream engagement.
- Test non-video formats first and only then video Shorts/Reels with original/licensed media and accurate synthetic-content labels.

## 5. Three measurable experiments (not promises)

E1 First-line hook test: 'The file came from the future' vs 'Who owns a human testimony?' on otherwise comparable organic posts over 4 weeks. Primary metrics outbound bio-link visits / reach and saves / reach; caveat audiences and channel algorithms differ.
E2 Destination: compare multilingual reading hub vs direct eligible English retailer link (Facebook only) by tagged clicks; don't assert purchase conversion absent seller attribution.
E3 Reader intent: 2–3 original bonus content angles (archive file, AI ethics memo, author research note); choose by landing-page opt-in rate after legal/technical setup.

Only alter one major variable at a time; small sample sizes warrant descriptive results rather than claimed statistical proof.

## 6. Weekly / monthly operations

Monday: check live social outcomes; published vs queued distinction, OpenSEO GSC clicks/impressions/CTR/query where data exists, Windsor connected datasets; retailer site visibility, new citations, user-owned reader-list growth.
Monthly: assess conversion proxies, cohort subscriber acquisition, actual verified KDP royalties and bank receipts, status of Book Two and translations. Refresh canonical reader-facing FAQs, outdated retailer links, metadata audit and next content batch. Do not convert rankings to sales.

## 7. Cash guardrails

Paid ads prohibited until actual royalties are received. No spending from personal money. After receipt calculate money available after tax, fees and committed obligations. Each paid campaign needs separate written author approval specifying network, maximum spend, duration, spend stop, eligible edition and evaluation metric.
Do not launch paid campaigns merely because royalties appear or on automation.

## 8. 30-60-90 day schedule

Days 1–30: Instagram nine-post queue once media URL solved; verified retail links; book listing QA; Goodreads/BookBub claim process; produce the first reader magnet draft; actual Search Console baseline.
Days 31–60: newsletter privacy/consent infrastructure (requires author/provider), first organic hook experiments, 10 targeted curator/reviewer proposals; German release only when confirmed. Evaluate click quality by language.
Days 61–90: evaluate funnel; curate consistent content, reader feedback, second book release readiness; measure genuine sales/receipts; only propose an ads experiment if capital and author approval exist.
