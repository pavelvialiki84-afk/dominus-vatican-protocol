## Selection framework (author's operational judgment, not measured econometric ranking)

1. Repeat purchase/read-through and 3+ standalone cases on repeat cast.
2. Low bespoke illustration/technical research burden compared with high-magic fantasy.
3. Ability to create meaningful original plots and an emotionally warm series identity.
4. English-first market positioning with separate storefront/pen-name and literary promises from DOMINUS.
5. Distinctive setting and protagonist; no accidental pastiche or reuse of existing characters.
6. Can run a low/no-spend discovery test before investing in editing/cover/marketing.

Preliminary choice: adult, no-graphic-violence, contemporary European village cozy mystery, with a practical archive/restoration angle. Avoid targeting vague entire 'mystery' category.

## Original concept — working creative proposal, NOT canon and NOT copyrighted manuscript

Series working label: **The Archivist's Village Mysteries**
English working author brand: TBD, choose distinctive pen name only after availability/rights verification.
Setting: fictional small river town in Central Europe with archive, restoration workshop, cafés, historical festivals; original town and cast.
Protagonist: Mara Vale, 42, former provenance researcher and paper conservator, inherited a small repair studio and a locked storeroom from an estranged aunt; recovering sense of community.
Recurring cast: pragmatic town librarian, retired local railway engineer, sharp-eyed pastry chef, a competent police detective who does not let Mara compromise evidence. No caricature of regional culture.
Emotional premise: in a town that treasures its history, everyone's most treasured object hides an inconvenient story.
Brand promise: fair-play puzzle + warm found-family relationships + satisfying solution in each book; family-safe violence (off-page), distinctive material-culture clues.

Book 1 working title: **The Last Page at Willow Quay** — an estate-sale ledger vanishes when the local antiquarian is found dead during the riverside paper festival. The apparently missing ledger page becomes a clue about forged provenance and inheritance. End must solve the death fairly and clearly.
Book 2: **A Death in the Clockmaker's Window** — a restored town clock and a missing maker's note connect to a murder amid a heritage festival. Resolve case independently, advance relationships.
Book 3: **The Secret Beneath the Binding** — an old account book with a deceptive binding leads to a theft-murder connected to the aunt's sealed inventory. Resolve individual case and close the season's family mystery.

Avoid overlapping DNA of DOMINUS; no Vatican, AI, scientific theology, conspiratorial architecture, secret mastermind or borrowed plot beats.

## Target manuscript specification — provisional

- Audience English-language adult cozy mystery readers, mobile ebook-first.
- Target 55k–70k words per novel after reviewing competitor text lengths; not a shortcut to final publishing quality.
- Series: 3 planned standalone cases, close with solution and relationship hook.
- Story blueprint: 30–40 beats, suspect matrix (at least 4 plausible), red herring causal audit, clue/payoff ledger, satisfying final reconstruction, realistic policing, dialogue-first scene drafting.
- Every manuscript professionally copy-edited/proofread as resources allow, human editorial gate and no auto-release.
- Book 1 outline must meet a puzzle fairness score, emotion arc, chapter progression and non-repetition.
- Cover: on-genre cozy visual, clear small-thumbnail typography; do not borrow bestselling cover artwork.

## Commercial hypotheses and decision gates

H1: cover + one-sentence promise + sample synopsis of European archive cozy is preferred vs standard bookshop/café cozy among actual readers.
H2: original companion object (fictional archival clue page) encourages opt-in more than generic author bio once GDPR-compliant subscription is possible.
H3: series proposition gets repeat interest; test newsletter click intention and early review signals without calling it actual purchase.

No prices claimed until checked. Provisional price experiments $3.99 vs $4.99 for English Kindle, eligible tiers and delivery costs confirmed at launch (not a set price or forecast).
Do not buy ads until actual book royalties are received and campaign budget separately approved.
No website/pen name/Goodreads identity automatically created; no manuscript or bookstore upload without approval.
Do not enter Select unless digitally exclusive for that series, reviewed against channel agreements.

## Phase gates

0. Source-verify 20 comparables: check actual individual Amazon/Kobo product pages, ISBN/ASIN, current retail price and length; capture sample book descriptions and cover conventions without copying protected content. Current benchmark file has listing-level evidence only.
1. **Concept gate**: author accepts reader promise, boundaries and brand separation.
2. **Story bible gate**: characters, town, three-book arcs and crime-solving rules.
3. **Outline gate**: 30–40 beats, no weak mystery or missing fair-play evidence.
4. **Draft gate**: create with human review, no summary masquerading as full text.
5. **Editorial/rights gate**: consistency, line editing, originality, KDP disclosure, EPUB QA.
6. **Financial gate**: real observed sales/royalties and actual receipts before commercial scale.

## Controls
Never represent market screenshots or income-survey category medians as publisher royalties. Never copy existing series plot, branding, trope-specific marketing copy or cover art. The purpose of initial work is to test demand, not to generate books at industrial volume without creative review.
