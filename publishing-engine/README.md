# DOMINUS PUBLISHING ENGINE 2.0
Effective: 2026-11-13 · Project owner: Pavel Velichii
Purpose: Operate two distinct lines: (A) DOMINUS premium intellectual Vatican/AI thriller; (B) Commercial Fiction Publishing pilot series, under distinct brands where appropriate.

## Research basis / boundaries
Coral Hart / PlotProse: market-first genre selection, reader-promise/trope mapping, beat-sheet planning, AI-assisted drafting/editing, human revision and proofreading, rapid release, newsletter, catalog-based economics. Her self-reported >$1m revenue is not independently verified; never adopt it as a target or forecast. Sources: https://www.straitstimes.com/life/arts/370-books-in-20-months-south-african-author-defends-her-use-of-ai , https://plotprose.com/about/ , https://coralhart.com/ventures , https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200644110 .

## A. Premium lane — DOMINUS
- Canon is locked per approved editorial architecture; preserve characters, timeline, theology, philology, and ambiguity.
- Manuscripts minimum length goals from author override rapid-release pressure.
- Mandatory five review passes: canon/plot; internal logic and chronology; technical/history/theology fact-check; line edit and literary prose; publication QA.
- Every localized version gets bilingual termbase and source-to-target completeness audit. No silent shortening of dialog or lore. Latin grammar and translation checked separately.
- Release only with author approval and final QA record.

## B. Commercial fiction pilot
- Start with ONE independently validated subgenre and a planned 3-book arc, not a portfolio of 21 pseudonyms.
- Track 20 comparable books: price, length, cover signals, subgenre, publication cadence, review *themes* and lawful public reader interest. Do not copy copyrighted plot, text or cover artwork.
- Define a 1-sentence audience promise, 3 reader expectations, 3 distinctive twists, book 1 hook and books 2-3 read-through.
- Draft synopsis, 30-40 scene beats, character state chart, constraints, timeline, and cliffhanger policy before prose.
- Human creative direction and review of every chapter; AI assistance permitted with audit trail, rights checks, truthful KDP AI disclosure.
- Require independent proofreading and ebook/device QA prior to release; no guaranteed quality from automated generation.
- Pilot viability gate: actual sales / sample retention / return rate / ratings evidence, where available; do not scale on invented metrics.

## Workflow and stage gates
0 MARKET: reader/competitor study with sources -> gate: real niche demand.
1 PRODUCT: one-line promise, tropes, differentiated concept, 3-book arc -> gate: author's signoff.
2 STORY BIBLE: canonical character, chronology, lore, forbidden contradictions, terminology -> gate: consistency.
3 PRODUCTION: scene checklist, chapter drafts, human review, revision -> gate: no unresolved continuity or generic prose.
4 QA: editorial five-pass, sensitivity/rights checks if relevant, proofread, EPUB/KDP Previewer, cover thumbnail test -> gate: checklists complete.
5 RELEASE: store metadata, truthful genres, AI content disclosure, rights, accessibility, translations, pricing signoff -> gate: author's go/no-go.
6 ACQUISITION: store page, website, reader magnet (only actual licensed excerpt), email permission, organic outreach -> gate: links and tracking verified.
7 OPTIMIZATION: monthly KDP units/royalties and ads (only with authorized exports), update campaigns based on evidence -> gate: positive contribution margin.
8 SCALE: greenlight next volume/series only when metrics and quality support.

## Hard constraints
- No fake reviews, ratings manipulation, review swaps, fake endorsements, market-spam or misleading claims of Vatican support.
- KDP policy: AI-generated TEXT/IMAGES/TRANSLATIONS must be disclosed even if substantially edited; AI-assisted original material does not require disclosure. Author responsible for copyrights and accuracy.
- No expenses, paid ads, uploading/publishing manuscript, outreach, prices, account changes without explicit approval.
- No fabricated KDP sales, traffic, ASINs or conversion; Amazon Author Central private data is not available to automations without a connection.
- Separate brands and mailing consent; EU GDPR-compliant opt-in before email collection.
