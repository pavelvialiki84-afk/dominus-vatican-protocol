2. The dramatic question is the authority to interpret and finalize testimony about a human being, not a simple AI-awakening mystery.
3. Preserve ambiguity between reconstruction, evidence, interpretation, institutional authority and personal correction.
4. Distinguish historic Vatican institutions and real scholarship from fictional settings, documents, technology and events. No Vatican endorsement.
5. AMRIEL, Vox Hominis, Nomen Nullum, LEX, Triplex and Seraphim are internal continuity checkpoints, not a marketing keyword dump. Keep spoilers out of opening copy.
6. Do not advertise future books, characters, dates, credentials, reviews, sales or awards without corresponding verified sources.
