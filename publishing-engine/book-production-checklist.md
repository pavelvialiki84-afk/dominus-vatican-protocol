# Publishing QA gates
## Research
- [ ] Audience promise and real comparable books verified with dated links
- [ ] Demand signal recorded and competing titles evaluated
- [ ] Cover/title conventions observed without imitation
## Editorial
- [ ] Author-approved concept and 3-book series arc
- [ ] Character bible, world bible and chronology locked
- [ ] Scene plan reviewed; each scene changes a choice, relationship or risk
- [ ] All chapters present, numbered and complete
- [ ] Repeated prose clichés removed; distinctive voice maintained
- [ ] Lore, theological and historical details checked at source
- [ ] All Latin translated directly below, with morphology verified
- [ ] Dialogue and plot content not silently reduced
- [ ] Human line edit and independent proofreading
## Localizations
- [ ] Names, factions, terminology and timeline matched against source
- [ ] No missing chapters/paragraphs; word-count discrepancy investigated
- [ ] Human German/English review and native-language fluency pass
## Production
- [ ] EPUB validation, TOC, links, fonts, cover dimensions and accessibility
- [ ] Kindle Previewer on phone/tablet/e-reader
- [ ] Correct title/series/contributors and valid publishing rights
- [ ] AI-generated text/images/translations declared to KDP where applicable
- [ ] Pricing and distribution approved by author
- [ ] ASIN / store page confirmed live before purchase-link announcement
## Growth / reputation
- [ ] Retail link goes to correct language/volume
- [ ] Site metadata, sample truthfulness and links checked
- [ ] Reader outreach ethical, GDPR email consent where applicable
- [ ] KDP metrics imported only from reports, not estimated web views
- [ ] Scale/no-scale decision signed off
