# DOMINUS | Reader acquisition without new spending
Working editorial and marketing plan (20 November 2026). Draft only; no posts have been published from this file.

## Goals and attribution
1. New qualified readers by language: EN, DE, FR, IT, ES.
2. First meaningful action: view authentic excerpt (currently available EN/DE/FR, IT/ES need real approved source text before excerpt claims).
3. Conversion: select edition -> direct Amazon store link. Track outbound visits only if analytics actually supports it.
4. Sales: only KDP/retailer reports; page impressions and Amazon rank are not purchases.

## Three distinct content pillars
**Archive & textual evidence** — What makes archival testimony credible? Facts about archival processes, followed by an explicit link to a fictional question in DOMINUS. Cite authentic sources for any factual claim.
**AI & institutional authority** — What may a machine reconstruct from testimony, and who can correct the record? Avoid promises of self-aware AI.
**The fictional case file** — Salvatore, archival locks, anomalous record and a short authentic opening quotation. No spoiler terms or invented manuscript quotations.

## Six reusable hooks, to localize faithfully after manuscript review
1. An archival record dated in the future. Who believes it?
2. The machine reconstructs testimony. Who has the right to correct it?
3. What if evidence becomes authority before anyone verifies it?
4. A dead witness. A system that can speak in their absence.
5. Faith meets a protocol that cannot understand doubt.
6. An institution demands certainty. A human life refuses to fit the record.
