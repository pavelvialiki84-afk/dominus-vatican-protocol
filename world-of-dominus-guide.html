WORLD OF DOMINUS · Reader Companion

Enter the World of DOMINUS: A Reader's Guide

A restricted Vatican vault. A file with no recorded origin. A future date. And a man who receives the evidence moments before the person who discovered it dies.

The beginning

It is 2035. Brother Salvatore Ventresca, an archivist involved with a classified project, finds something that should not exist in the system known as DOMINUS. A file carries a date months in the future, and the system's records cannot explain its creation.

Salvatore manages to pass evidence to Rafael Costa, a former consultant connected to the project. His death turns a technical anomaly into a human investigation.

The people

Brother Salvatore Ventresca understands the archive from inside. He knows how access, documentation and institutional procedure can protect evidence — or conceal it.

Rafael Costa is drawn back into a project from which he has become separated. His search leads beyond one suspicious file and toward a more fundamental problem: who has the authority to define a person when a machine can reconstruct a life from fragments?

Where the story travels

The Vatican is the opening pressure chamber, but the series reaches outward across a European landscape of archives, institutions and technological research. Each setting reflects a different kind of authority: religious, scientific, legal and personal.

How to read the series

Begin with Book One: Schism. It introduces the principal mystery, the central moral conflict and the system whose name gives the series its title. Read the opening chapter before exploring any companion material if you prefer to encounter the story without context.

This is a fictional literary universe. References to real cities and institutions do not imply endorsement or involvement in the events of the novel.
