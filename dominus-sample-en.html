DOMINUS: The Vatican Protocol

Book One: Schism

By Pavel Velichii. An intellectual techno-thriller set against the backdrop of the Vatican, hidden research and artificial intelligence.

A death in the archives and a record that should not exist ignite an investigation into memory, testimony and who has the last word about a human being.

The premise

In Rome in 2035, an impossible record appears in a sealed Vatican archive. The record bears a timestamp that lies months in the future. Soon afterward, the man who discovers it is dead. What begins as the investigation of an unexplained death becomes a confrontation between evidence and interpretation: what does a system actually know, and who is entitled to speak for a person who can no longer answer?

The theologian Rafael Costa and metrologist Luiza Barros follow a trail through historical archives, scientific infrastructure and restricted research. The questions that emerge are not limited to the technical capacities of artificial intelligence. They concern memory, testimony, responsibility and the temptation to treat a reconstruction of a life as the final account of that life.

A thriller about uncertainty

DOMINUS places scientific inquiry alongside theological and ethical argument without asking the reader to accept a supernatural explanation as a proven fact. An anomalous record may be evidence, deception or the result of assumptions no one has thought to challenge. Its investigators have to weigh possibilities while events make hesitation increasingly dangerous.

At the heart of the novel lies a deceptively simple question: if a machine can assemble the details of someone's identity, does that accuracy grant it authority over their story? The answer matters to investigators, institutions and anyone who wishes to retain the right to dispute what is said in their name.
