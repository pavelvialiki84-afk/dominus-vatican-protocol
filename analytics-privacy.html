Website analytics & privacy

English

We use Metricool to measure visits, page views and traffic sources on dominusbooks.com. The tracker sends the page URL (including any parameters), the referring page when available, and browser window dimensions to Metricool. The service receives the IP address with the connection. According to Metricool, its website tracker uses no cookies or identifiers stored on your device.

Our integration does not load when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. It does not record Amazon purchases or individual purchase-button clicks. Statistics start when tracking is installed; earlier visits cannot be recovered.
